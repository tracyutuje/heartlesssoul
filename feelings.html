Sometimes we cannot explain what is happening inside us. Explore the feelings we struggle to put into words.

Feeling Misunderstood

When people hear you but don't understand you.

OPEN THIS FEELING +

You do not have to explain every part of yourself to everyone. Being misunderstood does not make your feelings less valid.

🕯

Feeling Invisible

When your presence seems unnoticed.

OPEN THIS FEELING +

Your value is not measured by how much attention others give you. You matter, even when nobody notices your efforts.

🥀

Disappointment

When reality does not match what you hoped for.

OPEN THIS FEELING +

It is okay to feel disappointed. Give yourself time to understand what happened and decide what you want to do next.

🌑

Overthinking

When your thoughts become difficult to quiet.

OPEN THIS FEELING +

Not every question needs an answer tonight. Sometimes taking a break and focusing on the present is a good first step.

🖤

Feeling Unappreciated

When your efforts seem to go unnoticed.

OPEN THIS FEELING +

You deserve respect and appreciation. Remember to recognize your own effort, even when others forget to.

🌹

Quiet Strength

When you keep growing through difficult days.

OPEN THIS FEELING +

Strength is not pretending everything is fine. It can also mean asking for support, resting, and choosing to keep learning.
